Privacy Policy: The Snail and the Sun

Effective date: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game The Snail and the Sun ("the game", "we") handles, on Android (package com.zdravko.sunandsnail). The game is made by Zdravko Stoyanov, an independent developer.

Short version

  • The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no servers of its own. We do not receive any of your personal data.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device data (such as the advertising ID and IP address) to show and measure ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first, using Google's consent tool.
  • The optional "No ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

1. Information stored on your device

The game keeps a single save file in its private app storage on your phone. It contains:

  • your level progress (stars, best times, levels skipped with a rewarded ad);
  • which tip cards you have already seen and which snail shell you chose;
  • your settings (language, music, sound effects, vibration);
  • whether you own the "No ads" purchase.

This file never leaves your device through the game; we have no way to read it.

2. Information we collect

None. The game does not send any data to us. It does not use analytics, crash reporting or tracking services of its own, and it does not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information.

The game asks Android for these permissions only:

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and to talk to Google Play for purchases.
  • Vibrate: for the optional vibration effects (you can turn them off in Settings).
  • Advertising ID: added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK so ads can work (see section 3).

3. Third-party services

The game includes software development kits (SDKs) from Google. These SDKs send data directly to Google, not to us. Google's handling of that data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)

The game shows a full-screen ad between some levels (never during play, and not in the first levels) and, only if you choose it, a rewarded ad that lets you skip a level. To show, limit and measure these ads and to prevent fraud, Google's own disclosure says the SDK collects:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • ad and app interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information about app and SDK performance;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

Google says this data is encrypted in transit. Ads may be personalised or non-personalised depending on your consent and your device settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact menu depends on your phone).

Google User Messaging Platform (consent)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland (or in another region where Google requires it), the game shows Google's consent form before any ad is loaded. Ads are requested only after the form says they may be. Your choice is stored on your device by Google's SDK. To do this, the SDK sends some device information (such as IP address and device/app identifiers) to Google.

You can change your choice at any time: open Settings in the game and tap Ad privacy options. (This button appears whenever Google's consent tool says it is needed for your region.)

Google Play Billing ("No ads")

The one-time "No ads" purchase goes through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment and your payment details under the Google Payments Privacy Notice. The game only receives from Google Play, on your device, whether you own the product, so it can remove the ads between levels and restore the purchase after a reinstall. We do not run a server and do not receive your name, e-mail or payment information.

4. Legal basis (EEA/UK)

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, the processing described above is carried out by Google. Where personalised advertising requires consent, it is asked for through Google's consent form described in section 3. Non-personalised ads may still use data for purposes such as frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement, as described by Google.

5. Children

The game is a calm puzzle game for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not take part in Google Play's Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because younger players may still come across the game, every ad it requests is limited to Google's content rating G ("suitable for all audiences"). If you believe a child under 13 is using the game, a parent or guardian can turn off personalised ads in the device's settings (see section 7) or contact us (section 9).

6. How long data is kept

  • The save file stays on your device until you delete it (see section 7).
  • Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies; see How Google retains data.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete game data: uninstall the game, or go to Android Settings > Apps > The Snail and the Sun > Storage > Clear data. This deletes all progress and settings. A "No ads" purchase stays tied to your Google account and is restored by Restore purchases in the game's Settings.
  • Ad consent: change it any time with Ad privacy options in the game's Settings.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android's settings.
  • Data held by Google: see Google's privacy controls and Google's Privacy Policy.

Since we hold no personal data about you, we cannot access, correct or delete data held by Google on your behalf, but we are happy to help you find the right place.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Important changes will also be noted in the game's store listing.
