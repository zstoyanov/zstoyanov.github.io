# Moon Calendar (Лунен календар)

A Moon phase calendar for Android. Version 4.0 replaces the earlier "Moon Phases & Sky Events" app under the same Google Play listing (package `com.stoyanov.dev.android.moon`).

- **Today:** the Moon's phase, illumination, age, moonrise and moonset for your place.
- **Calendar:** the phases for every day of the month, with the exact times of new Moon, first quarter, full Moon and last quarter.
- **Sky events:** eclipses, planet conjunctions and meteor showers.
- **Traditional tips:** favourable days from folk tradition, always marked as tradition, not science.
- **Notifications:** optional reminders for phases and events, scheduled on your phone.
- **Offline:** all calculations run on your device; no account and no sign-in.
- Available in Bulgarian, English, German and Spanish.

The app is free and shows native ads from Google AdMob (marked "Ad"), with consent asked first where required.
